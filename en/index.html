FAQ

Questions parents ask

How is it different from regular flashcards?

Most flashcards only let kids look and listen. Kidvox adds “Say it”: your child holds the mic, says the word, and gets a pronunciation score right away, so they know if they got it and want to keep talking.

What age is it for?

Mainly toddlers aged 2–3 who are just starting to talk. Older kids, or children learning a second language, can use it to pick up new words and practice pronunciation too.

My toddler isn’t talking much yet. Can they still play?

Yes. Start by tapping cards to hear the words, then try the mic when your child is ready. Cards come one at a time, every word can be replayed, and a missed word only hears “Try saying it again.” It’s a practice game, not a speech therapy tool. Every child starts talking at their own pace, so if you’re concerned about your child’s language development, please talk to your pediatrician or a speech-language therapist.

Can my child practice another language?

Yes. Switch the language in Settings to Traditional Chinese, Spanish or Korean, and the words, audio and pronunciation scoring all switch (the app’s menus switch too).

How is the pronunciation score worked out? What if it’s too strict?

After your child says the word, speech recognition compares it with the correct pronunciation and gives a score from 0 to 100. Reach the passing score and the word counts as correct. Parents can change the Scoring Level in Settings: Easy (40), Medium (60, the default) or Hard (80). Kids just starting to talk can begin with Easy.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free to download. Four topics are free (Animals, Vehicles, City streets and Fruits), with “Say it” and “Find it” fully playable. The other topics unlock with a subscription, which also covers topics added later. Monthly and yearly plans are available, and the price in your currency is shown in the app.

Does it need the internet?

Pronunciation scoring in “Say it” needs a connection. Each topic’s cards and sounds download the first time it’s opened, then stay on the device.

Are my child’s recordings kept?

No. The mic only records while the button is held (up to 10 seconds). The clip is sent to a speech recognition service for scoring, and the temporary file is deleted from the device as soon as it’s done. We run no servers of our own and never keep, analyze or sell recordings. See the privacy policy.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets.
